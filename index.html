Eisenhower Matrix

Do — Urgent & Important Critical tasks needing immediate attention. Tackle these first.
Schedule — Important, Not Urgent High-value work. Block time for these before they become urgent.
Delegate — Urgent, Not Important Time-sensitive but low personal value. Hand off where possible.
Delete — Neither Distractions and low-value tasks. Drop or ignore them.
